Mission Brief

The Problem: Fire in Freefall

01 // Physics of Microgravity

When Buoyancy Disappears

On Earth, gravity-driven convection pulls cool oxygen in at the base of a fire and drives hot combustion gases upward into a flickering teardrop. In microgravity, hot gases no longer rise—flames form quiet, expanding spheres governed by slow molecular diffusion and subtle cabin ventilation currents.

02 // Spacecraft Crew Safety

Rethinking Detection & Suppression

Because microgravity flames burn at lower temperatures, produce far less visible soot, and drift along cabin airflow paths rather than rising toward ceiling sensors, Earth-calibrated smoke detectors and suppression protocols can miss critical early signatures. FlameMind equips researchers and mission planners with clear insights to safeguard crews living beyond Earth.
